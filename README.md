# Free Flo Box — Menstrual Product Usage Dashboard

A Tableau dashboard analyzing menstrual product usage for Free Flo Box, a
free menstrual-product access program, comparing two service locations
(St. Anne's Hill and South Park) across 2025.

## Research question

How does the usage of menstrual products differ based on reusability and
absorbency?

## Findings

Single-use products (pads and tampons) dominate usage at both locations —
reusable products (cups, period underwear) consistently make up under 1% of
items taken, despite a small uptick toward the end of the year. Demand for
single-use products is highest in summer/early fall at both sites. Regular
and Super absorbency pads/tampons see the most use, followed by Light and
Overnight; cups and period underwear are the least used category by a wide
margin.

The data points to a strong opportunity: educating users about reusable
options could shift some of that single-use demand and reduce the ongoing
cost/waste of restocking disposables.
